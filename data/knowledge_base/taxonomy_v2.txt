VEHICLE DAMAGE TAXONOMY v2.1
Auto Insurance Division
Last updated: 2024-01

§1. INTRODUCTION

This taxonomy defines the standardised vocabulary for vehicle damage classification used in 
automated and manual claims assessment. All damage codes in the ClaimLens system are derived 
from this taxonomy. Adjusters must use only codes defined in §2 when overriding AI assessments.

§2. DAMAGE CODE REFERENCE

§2.1 MINOR DAMAGE CODES
Minor damage is cosmetic in nature. The vehicle is fully drivable. Estimated cost: $200–$1,500.

FRONT_BUMPER_SCRATCH  : Surface scratching or scuffing on the front bumper fascia. 
                        No structural deformation. Repair: sanding + paint blend.
REAR_BUMPER_SCRATCH   : Same as above, rear bumper.
PAINT_TRANSFER        : Paint from another vehicle deposited on the surface. 
                        Repair: solvent removal or wet sanding.
SIDE_MIRROR_DAMAGE    : Side mirror housing cracked or mirror glass broken. 
                        Assembly replacement typically required.

§2.2 MODERATE DAMAGE CODES
Moderate damage requires panel repair or replacement. Cost: $1,500–$8,000.

FRONT_BUMPER_CRUSH    : Front bumper compressed or deformed. Fascia and absorber replacement.
REAR_BUMPER_CRUSH     : Rear bumper compressed or deformed. Fascia and absorber replacement.
HOOD_CREASE           : Hood panel has a crease or dent from impact. PDR or panel replacement.
TRUNK_LID_DENT        : Trunk lid dented. PDR or lid replacement.
DOOR_FL_DENT          : Front-left door dented. PDR or door skin replacement.
DOOR_FR_DENT          : Front-right door dented.
DOOR_RL_DENT          : Rear-left door dented.
DOOR_RR_DENT          : Rear-right door dented.
FENDER_FL_DENT        : Front-left fender dented. Fender replacement.
FENDER_FR_DENT        : Front-right fender dented.
WINDSHIELD_CRACK      : Windshield crack (not shatter). Star cracks < 6 inches may be repairable.
REAR_WINDOW_CRACK     : Rear window cracked. Full replacement required.
WHEEL_RIM_DAMAGE      : Alloy or steel rim bent or cracked. Rim replacement.

§2.3 SEVERE DAMAGE CODES
Severe damage involves major structural or safety systems. Cost: $8,000–$25,000.

HOOD_CRUSH            : Hood significantly crushed, indicating engine bay intrusion.
TRUNK_LID_CRUSH       : Trunk lid crushed, indicating boot area intrusion.
WINDSHIELD_SHATTER    : Complete windshield failure. Immediate safety risk.
UNDERCARRIAGE_DAMAGE  : Damage to underbody, floor pan, or exhaust. Requires inspection jack.
AIRBAG_DEPLOYED       : One or more airbags deployed. Full SRS system reset/replacement.
STRUCTURAL_DAMAGE     : Frame rail, A/B/C pillar, or unibody deformation detected.

§2.4 TOTAL LOSS CODES
FLOOD_DAMAGE          : Water ingress above door sill. ECU, wiring, upholstery involved.

§3. SEVERITY THRESHOLDS

§3.1 MINOR: All damage codes are in §2.1 only. No safety systems involved. Cost < $1,500.

§3.2 MODERATE: One or more §2.2 codes present. No structural damage. Cost $1,500–$8,000.
    A claim is escalated to Moderate if:
    - ≥2 panels are damaged, OR
    - Any glass component is damaged, OR
    - Cost estimate exceeds $1,500

§3.3 SEVERE: One or more §2.3 codes present. Structural integrity may be compromised.
    Automatic Severe triggers:
    - STRUCTURAL_DAMAGE present
    - AIRBAG_DEPLOYED present
    - ≥3 panel codes across §2.2–§2.3
    - Cost estimate exceeds $8,000

§3.4 TOTAL LOSS: Repair cost > 75% of Actual Cash Value (ACV), OR structural damage 
    makes safe repair impractical, OR FLOOD_DAMAGE code present.

§4. MULTI-VEHICLE AND MULTI-ANGLE ASSESSMENTS

When multiple images are submitted for a single claim:
1. Each image is assessed independently.
2. Damage codes are merged (union of all detected codes).
3. Severity is determined by the highest individual severity across all images.
4. Bounding boxes are stored per-image.

§5. FRAUD INDICATORS

The fraud_flag should be set when:
- Damage pattern inconsistent with stated accident type (e.g., rear damage from head-on)
- Damage appears staged (bilateral symmetric impact, no road surface contact marks)
- Policy inception < 30 days before claim date
- Vehicle ACV < estimated repair cost but not classified as Total Loss
- Damage type inconsistent with vehicle age/mileage profile

§6. PRE-EXISTING DAMAGE

Pre-existing damage must be identified at policy inception via inspection photos.
If pre-existing damage is detected in claim images:
1. Flag for manual adjuster review.
2. Split assessment: pre-existing (excluded) vs. new damage (claimable).
3. Document with PRIOR_DAMAGE note in the override reason field.
