# PROPERTY CATASTROPHE INSURANCE & REMOTE DAMAGE APPRAISAL STANDARD
Document ID: PROP-INS-MMB-2025-V1
Scope: Commercial & Residential Property Insurance, Disaster Assessment, and Aerial Claim Processing
Applicability: Hurricane, Flood, Wildfire, Earthquake, Tornado, Storm Surge, Roof and Structural Collapse

================================================================================
1. INSURED PROPERTY & DISASTER PERILS
================================================================================

1.1 Insured Subject
Structures, roofing systems, foundations, exterior envelopes, and permanent fixtures situated within the declared policy location.

1.2 Covered Catastrophe Perils:
- Tropical Cyclones & Hurricanes: Sustained high-velocity wind, barometric pressure differentials, and wind-driven rain penetration.
- Flash Floods & Inundation: Surface water runoff, riverine breaches, and coastal storm surge overtopping foundation sills.
- Wildfires & Conflagration: Direct flame impingement, radiant heat combustion, and airborne ember ignition.
- Earthquakes & Seismic Shock: Ground acceleration causing foundation shear, masonry cracking, and load-bearing failure.
- Severe Convective Storms: Tornadic wind uplift, localized microbursts, and large-diameter hail impacts.

================================================================================
2. FIVE-STEP SATELLITE / AERIAL PROPERTY APPRAISAL PROTOCOL
================================================================================

Step 1 — Determine Whether a Disaster Occurred:
Perform comparative analysis between pre-disaster baseline imagery and post-event surveillance to verify catastrophic impact (Yes / No).

Step 2 — Identify Disaster Type:
Classify the environmental mechanism based on signature visual evidence:
- Hurricane / Cyclone: Widespread roof envelope stripping, distributed debris scatter aligned with wind vectors, standing coastal water.
- Inundation / Flood: Silt deposition, reflective water pooling across parcels, submersion of transit infrastructure.
- Wildfire: Complete structural footprint ash footprint, blackened foliage boundaries, perimeter firebreaks.
- Earthquake: Diagonal wall shear failure, roof pancake collapse, ground rupture fissures.

Step 3 — Enumerate Total House / Structure Count:
Identify the total number of built footprints within the delineated appraisal sector.

Step 4 — Enumerate Damaged House / Structure Count:
Count individual structures exhibiting envelope breach, missing roof decking, wall collapse, or complete structural leveling. Calculate the Area Damage Ratio:
Damage Ratio = (Damaged Structure Count) / (Total Structure Count)

Step 5 — Insurance Claim Decision & Rationale:
- No Compensation: No catastrophe detected (Damage Ratio = 0%), or damage strictly restricted to uninsurable landscaping.
- Partial Compensation: Damage Ratio between 1% and 49%. Individual structures retain core load-bearing integrity; repairs involve roof reroofing, siding replacement, or interior dry-out.
- Full Compensation: Damage Ratio >= 50%, or community infrastructure condemned as a constructive total loss. Policy limits disbursed under Catastrophe Endorsement clauses.

================================================================================
3. PROPERTY DAMAGE SEVERITY TIERS
================================================================================

Severity Tier   Damage Ratio    Typical Structural Condition                    Claim Settlement
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minor           < 10%           Missing shingles, fascia denting, cosmetic scuff Repair estimate minus policy deductible
Moderate        10% – 49%       Partial roof uplift, wall penetration, water     Itemized structural repair indemnity
Severe          50% – 79%       Major framing failure, interior wall collapse    Substantial loss settlement
Total Loss      >= 80%          Structural foundation scrape, complete collapse  100% Policy limit + debris removal
