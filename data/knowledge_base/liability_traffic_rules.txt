# TRAFFIC LIABILITY ASSESSMENT & ACCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION STANDARD
Document ID: LIAB-INS-MMB-2025-V1
Scope: Auto Liability Claims, Multi-Vehicle Collision Adjudication, Dashcam Video Analysis, and Fault Attribution
Applicability: Intersection Collisions, Rear-End Impacts, Lane Changing, Pedestrian Incidents, Highway and Urban Scenes

================================================================================
1. SIX-STEP DASHCAM / SCENE ACCIDENT EVALUATION PROTOCOL
================================================================================

All auto liability and dashcam accident assessments must strictly execute the six-step sequence established in the INS-MMBench Liability Benchmark:

Step 1 — Prevailing Weather Conditions:
Identify atmospheric lighting and precipitation:
- Sunny / Clear: Optimal dry pavement traction, clear optical line of sight.
- Rainy / Wet: Reduced pavement friction coefficient, hydrodynamic spray, glare reflections.
- Snowy / Icy: Severely degraded stopping distance, surface snow packing, lane marking obliteration.
- Foggy / Mist: Restricted sight distance, low optical contrast.

Step 2 — Operating Scene Environment:
Identify the contextual roadway environment:
- Highway / Freeway: Controlled access, high travel velocities, multi-lane divided traffic.
- Urban: High density, street-side parked vehicles, visual clutter, commercial driveways.
- Rural: Undivided two-lane corridors, unlighted shoulders, agricultural crossings.
- Mountain: Grade variations, tight radius turns, rock-face visual obstructions.
- Tunnel: Artificial lighting transition, confined clearance barriers.

Step 3 — Roadway Linear Geometry:
Classify the layout of the collision locus:
- Arterials: Straight multi-lane thoroughfares.
- Intersection: At-grade crossing governed by traffic control signals, stop signs, or roundabouts.
- T-Junction: Three-way perpendicular intersection with defined right-of-way hierarchy.
- Curve: Horizontal radius requiring lateral deceleration and steering deflection.
- Ramp: Acceleration or deceleration connector lanes.

Step 4 — Accident Occurrence Verification:
Confirm whether an impact event, vehicle contact, or evasive roadway departure occurred (Yes / No).

Step 5 — Proximate Accident Cause:
Synthesize the primary physical mechanism of the collision into a concise factual summary:
- Failure to yield right-of-way during unprotected left turn.
- Inadequate following distance resulting in forward vehicle rear-end impact.
- Sudden lateral drift / lane change into an occupied blind-spot lane.
- Failure to observe opposing crossing traffic when entering from an unsignalized side road.
- Disregard of red traffic signal or posted stop sign control.

Step 6 — Main Responsible Party & Fault Allocation:
Determine liability allocation under comparative negligence rules:
- Ego-Car Primary: Driver of the recording vehicle committed the primary moving violation or failed to yield.
- Other Car Primary: Striking third-party vehicle violated traffic controls or made an illegal maneuver.
- Pedestrian Primary: Pedestrian crossed active arterial outside crosswalk directly into moving path.
- Both (Comparative Fault): Both drivers contributed through concurrent negligence (e.g. mutual intersection entry without clearance).
